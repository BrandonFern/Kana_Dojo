Study Tips for Real Fluency
🌅 Daily 10 minutes crushes occasional 2-hour sessions. Consistency is the whole game.
⚔️ Brute Force sets — the familiarisation phase is NOT cheating. Looking at meanings first is proven neuroscience.
🔁 Spaced repetition — the cumulative review after each set ensures older kanji don't fade.
✍️ Write by hand while drilling — muscle memory dramatically speeds recognition.
🔗 Use radicals as anchors — 木 (tree) appears in 森 (forest), 林 (woods), 校 (school). Spot the pattern.
📅 Target: 46 hiragana in ~1 week · 46 katakana in week 2 · 1 BF kanji set per day from week 3.
